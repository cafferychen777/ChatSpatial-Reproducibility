M-DECONV rules: is the OSCC leading-edge enrichment the same under CARD, RCTD and FlashDeconv?
Written 2026-10-09, before any of the runs below were started.

1. The claim being tested
The manuscript (main.tex, OSCC Step 2; Supplementary Table 1) reports that the Leading Edge (LE) is
enriched for macrophages (11/12 samples) and fibroblasts/CAFs (10/12) relative to the Tumor Core (TC),
with sample-level Wilcoxon signed-rank tests over 11 patients (Sample 6 has no LE spots):
macrophage q = 0.003, fibroblast q = 0.005, CAF q = 0.027, endothelial q = 0.003 (LE), cancer q = 0.003 (TC).
The text calls the strongest signals "macrophage and fibroblast LE enrichment".

Provenance: the per-spot proportions behind Fig. 2d/e and Supplementary Table 1 of the first
submission (server_results/per_sample_tc_le_enrichment.csv = source_data/Fig2d_enrichment.csv, identical
values) were produced by FlashDeconv in the original case-study pipeline. This run compares CARD, RCTD
and FlashDeconv on the same spots and labels.

2. Fixed inputs (identical for all three methods)
- Spots and TC/LE labels: the 12 files GSE208253/processed_h5ad/s{1..12}_processed.h5ad, column
  obs["tc_le_region"] (TC, LE, Other), exactly as used for the manuscript. No re-clustering.
- Spatial counts: layers["counts"] of those files (raw UMI counts after the manuscript's QC).
- Reference: Puram et al. GSE103322_HNSCC_all_data.txt.gz, cell-type labels built exactly as in
  03_run_analysis.py (row "non-cancer cell type"; label 0 -> "Cancer"; 10 labels).
  The GEO matrix is log2(TPM/10 + 1). CARD and RCTD in ChatSpatial 1.5.5 require integer counts,
  so the reference given to all three methods is round(2^x - 1), i.e. TPM/10 rounded to integers.
  The same integer matrix is given to every method.
- Software: ChatSpatial 1.5.5 (conda env chatspatial_v155), called through
  chatspatial.tools.deconvolution.deconvolve_spatial_data with schema defaults except method,
  reference_data_id and cell_type_key:
    CARD: minCountGene 100, minCountSpot 5 (defaults; same as Arora et al.).
    RCTD: R backend (spacexr), mode "full" (default for Visium); 1.5.5 returns per-spot
          proportions normalized to sum to one. ChatSpatial drops reference types with fewer than
          25 cells for RCTD, so "-Fibroblast" (CAF, 18 cells) and "myocyte" (19 cells) are not
          estimated by RCTD. This is a property of RCTD, not a failure.
    FlashDeconv: sketch_dim 512, lambda_spatial auto, n_hvg 2000, n_markers_per_type 50 (defaults).
- Anchor run (secondary, not part of the decision): FlashDeconv via ChatSpatial 1.5.5 with the
  log-scale reference exactly as the manuscript pipeline used it, to check that the reported numbers
  reproduce under 1.5.5.

3. Statistics (identical to the manuscript pipeline, applied separately to each method)
Per sample and cell type: two-sided Mann-Whitney U on spot proportions, LE spots vs TC spots;
log2FC = log2((mean_LE + 1e-6) / (mean_TC + 1e-6)); BH-FDR over all sample x cell-type tests of that
method (tests with all-zero proportions are excluded); a sample is LE-enriched if q < 0.05 and
log2FC > 0.25, TC-enriched if q < 0.05 and log2FC < -0.25. Counts are reported out of 12 samples.
Patient level: two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the nonzero per-sample log2FC over the 11
samples with LE spots (Sample 6 excluded); BH-FDR over cell types within the method; direction from
the median log2FC.

4. Decision rule ("the leading-edge enrichment")
Primary quantity: LE enrichment of macrophages and of fibroblasts (label "Fibroblast").
A cell type's LE enrichment is REPRODUCED under a method if the patient-level signed-rank q < 0.05 and
the median log2FC > 0.
- CONSISTENT: reproduced for both macrophages and
  fibroblasts under all three methods. Fill M-DECONV with "reproduced".
- PARTLY CONSISTENT: reproduced for one of the two cell types under all three methods, or for both
  under two of the three. Do not fill; report the rewrite naming what held and what did not.
- INCONSISTENT: anything else. Any significant TC-direction result (q < 0.05, median log2FC < 0) for
  macrophages or fibroblasts under any method is reported as a contradiction.
Secondary (reported, not used for the decision): number of LE-enriched samples per method and
whether it reaches the manuscript's consensus threshold (>= 10 of 12); cancer-cell TC direction;
endothelial and CAF results (CAF not available under RCTD); per-sample sign agreement of log2FC between
methods; spot-level Pearson correlation of proportions between methods for each shared cell type.

5. What is not changed after seeing results
The labels, the reference construction, the parameters, the tests, the thresholds and the decision
rule above. If a method fails to run on a sample, the failure is reported and the sample is counted as
not LE-enriched for that method; the decision is then made on the samples that ran, and this is stated.
